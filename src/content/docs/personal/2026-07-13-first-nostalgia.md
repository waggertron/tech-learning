---
title: "First Nostalgia"
description: "A short poem about cave paintings, memory, beauty, and the first impulse to make art."
date: 2026-07-13
tags: [poetry, personal]
---

At  
some point  
someone decided  
there's things  
that should be seen again.  
so they painted  
on a sullen cavern wall.  
imagine  
how beautiful  
they must have been.  
someone  
worth inventing art for  

-- W.w.
