---
title: "Falling Over"
description: "A short poem about mutual support, shared instability, and standing because someone else stands with you."
date: 2026-07-13
tags: [poetry, personal]
---

We don’t even\
have to be pillars,\
we can settle\
on just being\
a tepee.\
Constantly\
falling over,\
yet,\
always standing\
with each other

W.w.
